## What FIRESTARTERS Game Mechanics Actually Ask of You

FIRESTARTERS is a fast-paced arcade movement shooter built around one rule: never stand still. Studio Kerosene's Early Access release, which launched on October 28, 2026, pairs rocket-skate traversal with arena FPS gunplay and a scoring system that rewards speed as much as survival. Understanding the mechanics matters because the game deliberately removes the safety nets most shooters rely on — there are no health, armor, or ammo pickups anywhere in a STEEL Arena, so your movement and your trigger discipline are the only things keeping the run alive.

Below is what the official Steam listing and hands-on coverage confirm about how the systems fit together, plus which parts are still in flux during Early Access.

## Movement Is the Core System, Not a Bonus

The official description of Kera's kit is unusually literal: a performance-enhancing STEEL Battlesuit, a custom pair of rocket skates, and a curated suite of arena FPS weapons. The arenas themselves are described as skateparks rather than cover-shooter rooms, which tells you how the level design is meant to be read.

Confirmed traversal verbs listed by the developer:

- **Skate** — the baseline state of high-speed travel through an arena
- **Slide** — a low, fast option for changing lines and slipping under threats
- **Grind** — traversal along rails built into the environments
- **Ground pound** — a vertical attack option that doubles as a way to return to ground level

Hands-on coverage from [Coloured Static Press](https://colouredstaticpress.com/firestarters-hands-on-preview-steam-early-access-studio-kerosene/) describes arenas of rails, ramps, and sharp turns that are "designed to be traversed rather than treated as rooms in which to take cover." The same preview notes a synchronization meter appearing in the HUD, though the developer has not published a detailed breakdown of what feeds it.

What this means in practice: chaining movement options is how you avoid the "savage mechanized claws" of the android enemies, and it is also how you line up the next target. Momentum is the resource the game is actually asking you to manage.

## Combat: Six Weapons and a Self-Sufficient Gunplay Loop

Steam's listing states plainly that players get an arsenal of six weapons. It does not name them, list their ammo types, or explain unlock progression, so treat any weapon tier list you see online as speculation until Studio Kerosene publishes details.

The more interesting mechanical wrinkle is resource economy. A STEEL Arena contains no health pickups, no armor pickups, and no ammunition drops. Instead, contestants take what they need from defeated androids. Lead developer Alex Harte framed it to [MonsterVine](https://monstervine.com/2026/10/firestarters-steam-early-access-october-28/) this way: there are no safety nets in the arenas of Steel, and hunting is the path to survival.

That single design choice reshapes everything else:

- **Aggression is defensive.** Backing off to recover is not an option, because recovery comes from kills.
- **Accuracy has an economic cost.** Wasted shots cannot be topped up from a crate on the floor.
- **Routing matters.** Where you travel next is partly a decision about which enemies you can reach and convert into resources.

Because the base combat systems shipped complete in Episode One, this loop is functional now — though the developer notes the systems remain subject to change as new content is developed.

## Storms, Scoring, and the Arcade Mode Loop

A combat encounter in FIRESTARTERS is called a Storm. Clearing one is described by the developer as the minimum bar, not the goal — spectators in the fiction demand competitors play better, slay faster, and score higher.

The structure works like this:

1. You clear a Storm for the first time in Story Mode.
2. That Storm becomes available to replay in Arcade Mode.
3. Arcade runs feed an official high score, tracked against your personal best, your Steam friends, and global leaderboards.

Hands-on impressions describe the HUD tracking remaining enemies, score, and multipliers during a run, and note that the scoring system gives a genuine reason to retry an arena rather than treat it as a checklist of targets. The same preview flags a readability concern: in the striking red industrial arenas, a dense HUD and multiple simultaneous warnings can compete for your attention once the pace ramps up.

## Story Mode, Episode One, and Early Access Caveats

Mechanics do not exist in a vacuum here. FIRESTARTERS frames its arenas with visual-novel-style cutscenes covering Kera, a former delinquent from Okinawa now fighting for a prisoner team in a syndicate-run bloodsport. Story Mode is where you unlock Arcade Mode content by clearing Storms.

Scope, as stated by the developer on [the official FIRESTARTERS Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3733710/FIRESTARTERS/):

- Episode One is the current Early Access build and includes the opening of Story Mode plus unlockable Arcade Mode.
- Four episodes are planned before version 1.0, with the remaining three arriving as free updates for Early Access owners.
- The studio estimates 18 to 24 months in Early Access, and says the number of episodes may change.
- The price will rise as content ships; the Steam listing showed $9.99 at the time of writing, with UK coverage citing £6.99 at launch.

One technical note that touches gameplay feel: Studio Kerosene built the game on a custom in-house engine and states it avoids AI upscaling and shader compilation stutter. Those are the developer's claims rather than independently verified findings.

## FAQ

**Do FIRESTARTERS game mechanics include health or ammo pickups?**
No. The developer states there are no health, armor, or ammunition pickups in a STEEL Arena. You recover what you need by defeating android enemies, which is the mechanic that pushes players toward aggressive, forward-moving play instead of retreating to heal.

**How many weapons do you get in FIRESTARTERS?**
Six. The Steam listing confirms an arsenal of six weapons but does not name them or detail their stats, so specifics beyond that count are not yet officially documented.

**What is a Storm in FIRESTARTERS?**
A Storm is the game's term for a combat encounter. You clear it first in Story Mode, then replay it in Arcade Mode to set an official high score that can be compared against your own best, your Steam friends, and global leaderboards.

**Do FIRESTARTERS game mechanics change during Early Access?**
Yes, potentially. The developer says the base gameplay, story, and combat systems are complete and functional but subject to change as new content is developed, and three more episodes are planned as free updates before version 1.0.
