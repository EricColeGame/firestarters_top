## Momentum First: The Core FIRESTARTERS Mindset

FIRESTARTERS is an arcade movement shooter that punishes standing still, and the fastest way to improve is to stop treating its arenas like cover shooters. The game launched into Steam Early Access on October 28, 2026, with Episode One, and its rules are unusually strict: there are no health, armor, or ammunition pickups anywhere in a STEEL Arena. Everything you need comes from the androids you kill, so survival and aggression are the same skill. These FIRESTARTERS tips focus on what the game actually asks of you — keep moving, keep killing, and keep the score climbing — rather than on guesswork about a build that is still changing.

Because Episode One is an Early Access release, treat every tip here as a snapshot of the current build. Studio Kerosene says the base gameplay, story, and combat systems are complete and functional, but explicitly notes they are subject to change as new content ships.

## Movement Tips for Rocket Skates and Arena Traversal

Kera's kit is built around a STEEL Battlesuit and a custom pair of rocket skates, and the official description lists four verbs you should learn early: skate, slide, grind, and ground pound. Each one exists to keep you off the ground and out of the mechanized claws of the android horde.

- **Skate to travel, not to reposition slightly.** Speed is your defense. Long, committed lines across an arena beat short nervous hops.
- **Slide and grind to convert existing momentum.** Rails, ramps, and sharp turns are traversal tools, not decoration — use them to keep a run flowing instead of resetting your pace.
- **Ground pound as a deliberate choice.** It is a committed action, so pick a moment when the surrounding threats are already thinned out.
- **Learn the layout before you learn the score.** A hands-on preview from [Coloured Static Press](https://colouredstaticpress.com/firestarters-hands-on-preview-steam-early-access-studio-kerosene/) noted that both the bright training spaces and the red industrial arenas are designed to be traversed rather than used as cover.

That same preview described FIRESTARTERS as a game that "wants momentum," where a clean run means lining up the next threat while sustaining pace. If you find yourself stopping to aim carefully from a safe corner, you are probably playing against the design.

## Survival Tips: Hunt to Restock in STEEL Arenas

The single biggest adjustment for most players is the resource economy. Steam's store page is blunt about it: "If the world doesn't give, you'll take." Lead developer Alex Harte framed it similarly to press, saying there are no safety nets in STEEL and that hunting is the path to survival.

Practical implications:

- **Push toward enemies when you are low, not away from them.** Retreating buys distance but no resources.
- **Rotate through your six weapons.** With no ammo drops, the arsenal is a toolkit to be cycled rather than a single favorite gun you hoard.
- **Finish fights cleanly.** A drawn-out engagement drains you faster than a quick, committed push that ends with a fresh supply.
- **Use vertical space to break line of sight.** Claws are dangerous in a straight line; ramps and platforms are how you reset an engagement on your terms.

## Scoring Tips: Build Pace, Then Chase the Leaderboard

Clearing a Storm is only the entry fee. The game tracks remaining enemies, score, and multipliers while pushing you through large arenas, and spectators reward speed as much as survival. Faster kills and higher scores are the point.

Here is a sensible progression:

1. **Clear each Storm once in Story Mode.** This is what unlocks it for repeat play.
2. **Return in Arcade Mode.** Steam's listing states that after finishing a Storm in the story, you can replay it in Arcade Mode to set an official high score.
3. **Chase your own best before chasing anyone else's.** Beat your personal record, then your Steam friends, then look at the global leaderboard.
4. **Clean up your route, not just your aim.** Momentum-based scoring rewards a run with fewer dead stops, so shaving a bad turn is often worth more than a slightly faster kill.

## FAQ

**Does FIRESTARTERS have health or ammo pickups?**
No. There are no health, armor, or ammunition pickups in a STEEL Arena. You recover what you need from defeated androids, which is why aggressive play is the safest play.

**How do I unlock Arcade Mode?**
Complete a Storm for the first time in Story Mode. That Storm then becomes available in Arcade Mode, where you can set an official high score and compete on global leaderboards.

**Are these FIRESTARTERS tips still valid after future updates?**
Mostly, because they describe the game's core design — momentum, hunting for resources, and score chasing. Exact balance, HUD behavior, and content will change as the remaining three episodes ship, so re-check the Steam page after major patches.

**Is FIRESTARTERS finished?**
No. It entered Early Access on October 28, 2026, with Episode One, which includes the opening of Story Mode and the unlockable Arcade Mode. The developer estimates 18 to 24 months before version 1.0.
