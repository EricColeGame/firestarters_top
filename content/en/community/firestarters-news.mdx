## FIRESTARTERS News: Episode One Is Live on Steam

The biggest FIRESTARTERS news of the past week is simple: the game is out. Studio Kerosene launched FIRESTARTERS into Steam Early Access on October 28, 2026, at $9.99, giving players the first of four planned content episodes. That matters because the launch build is not a demo or a teaser — it ships with the opening chunk of Story Mode and a fully unlockable Arcade Mode tied to global leaderboards.

If you are deciding whether to jump in now or wait, the honest answer lives in the developer's own Early Access disclosure. The base gameplay, story, and combat systems are described as complete and functional, but the studio expects to keep building for roughly 18 to 24 months before version 1.0. Two hotfixes have already landed since launch, which tells you the team is actively patching rather than sitting on the release.

You can see the current build, price, and system requirements on the [official FIRESTARTERS Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3733710/FIRESTARTERS/).

## What You Actually Play in Episode One

FIRESTARTERS is a fast-paced arcade movement shooter with visual novel story cutscenes. You play as Kera, a former delinquent street kid from Okinawa who becomes the designated fighter for the Firestarters, the newest prisoner team in STEEL — a livestreamed Japanese gladiator bloodsport run by a crime syndicate.

The movement is the hook. Kera's STEEL Battlesuit and custom rocket skates turn each arena into a skatepark, and you are expected to skate, slide, grind, and ground pound your way around mechanized enemies rather than hold a corner. Six weapons make up the arsenal.

Resource management is deliberately unusual:

- There are no health, armor, or ammunition pickups in a STEEL arena.
- You resupply by taking what you need from defeated androids.
- Clearing a fight is only half the job — faster kills and higher scores satisfy the audience.

"There are no safety nets in the arenas of Steel," lead developer Alex Harte told MonsterVine. "Hunting is the path to survival."

Once you finish a Storm in the story, it unlocks in Arcade Mode, where runs are scored and ranked against friends and the global leaderboard.

### A Custom Engine and a No-AI Claim

Studio Kerosene says FIRESTARTERS runs on a fully custom, in-house engine built from the ground up, pitched as a way to avoid shader compilation stutter and heavy install sizes. The studio also states that no generative AI tools were used in the game's creation.

Two disclaimers are worth flagging before purchase: the game carries a photosensitivity warning for flashing and stroboscopic effects, and its mature content description covers blood, intense violence, strong language, mature and suggestive themes, and tobacco use.

## Patch Notes: What Changed on October 28 and October 31

Both post-launch updates are small, targeted hotfixes posted to the [FIRESTARTERS Steam News Hub](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3733710). Neither adds content.

| Build | Date | Headline fixes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.2.2.1 | Oct 28, 2026 | Crash when playing with Russian language enabled; visual novel text word-wrapping bug |
| 0.2.2.7 | Oct 31, 2026 | Startup crash when the audio engine could not initialize a default playback device; input rebinding breaking on some keyboard layouts (Japanese keyboards cited); updated Japanese strings |

The October 31 patch adds a useful fallback: if the default audio device fails, the game now tries other onboard devices, and if none initialize it still boots — just silently. The developers say re-initialization attempts may come later.

Worth noting for context: the public demo was patched repeatedly from February through March 2026 (builds in the 0.1.x line), covering everything from Vulkan crash fixes to input rebinds and purchase-phase behavior. Those demo notes are historical and do not describe the current Early Access build.

## The Roadmap: Four Episodes, Then 1.0

The developer's Early Access plan is episodic rather than feature-flagged. FIRESTARTERS is intended to stay in Early Access until the final story episode ships, which will be marked as version 1.0 — an estimated 18 to 24 months from launch.

- Four "Episodes" are planned before 1.0, though the studio notes the number may change.
- Episode One is the current build; it includes the first part of Story Mode plus Arcade Mode.
- Interim updates will also revise and improve existing content.
- No release schedule for Episodes Two through Four has been announced.

Pricing follows the same trajectory. Studio Kerosene plans to raise the price gradually as content and features ship, and anyone who buys during Early Access gets all pre-1.0 content updates free. That is the practical argument for buying early if the current build already appeals to you.

## Languages, Specs, and Community Feedback

FIRESTARTERS ships with English, Japanese, and Russian interface and subtitle support; the store listing shows no full-audio language. Steam Deck compatibility is listed as Unknown, so handheld performance is unverified by the developer.

The PC requirements are modest and explicitly not final:

- **Minimum:** Windows 10, 4-core CPU (Intel i3 or AMD Ryzen 3), 2 GB RAM, DirectX 12, 1 GB storage.
- **Recommended:** Windows 11, 6-core CPU (Intel i5 or AMD Ryzen 5), 2 GB RAM, 1 GB storage.

On the community side, the studio has publicly pledged to read and consider feedback from the Steam Forums and any other channel it can find, official or not — a commitment worth holding them to as the episode cadence unfolds. MonsterVine's [launch coverage of the rocket-skate combat and episode structure](https://monstervine.com/2026/10/firestarters-steam-early-access-october-28/) remains a useful independent summary of what was promised pre-launch.
