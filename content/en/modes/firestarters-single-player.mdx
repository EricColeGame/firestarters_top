## Is FIRESTARTERS Single Player?

Yes. FIRESTARTERS is built as a single player experience. Studio Kerosene's arcade movement shooter launched in Steam Early Access on October 28, 2026, and its official Steam listing carries the Single-player category — with no co-op or multiplayer tag alongside it. Everything you shoot, skate, and grind through is designed for one player: Kera, a prisoner competing in the televised bloodsport STEEL.

That matters because the game's marketing leans hard on speed and competition. Leaderboards, high scores, and a livestreamed gladiator setting can look like multiplayer bait, but the competition is asynchronous. You are chasing other players' scores, not fighting them in the same arena.

## What Single Player Content Is in Early Access Right Now

The current Early Access build is the initial release, consisting of Episode One. According to the developers, Episode One includes two distinct pieces of solo content:

- **Story Mode, Part One** — the opening slice of the main narrative, told through visual novel style cutscenes between combat encounters.
- **Arcade Mode** — an unlockable mode that lets you replay completed arenas and compete for high scores on global leaderboards.

Studio Kerosene states that the base gameplay, story, and combat systems are already complete and functional in this build, though they remain subject to change as new episodes ship. The full version is planned to reach 1.0 after four total "Episodes," with an estimated Early Access window of roughly 18 to 24 months — a number the studio notes may change.

### Story Mode: The Narrative Spine

You play as Kera, a former delinquent from the back alleys of Okinawa who becomes the designated fighter for the Firestarters, the newest team of prisoner-contestants in STEEL. The sport is owned and operated by a Japanese crime syndicate, and the story frames her run as a fight for her friends' freedom and atonement for her past.

Story Mode is where the visual novel cutscenes live. If you are coming to FIRESTARTERS for character writing between fights rather than pure score attack, this is the mode that carries it — and it is the part of the game that will grow with each new episode.

### Arcade Mode: Solo Score Attack

Arcade Mode is where the single player loop becomes competitive. After you clear a Storm for the first time in the story, you can return to it in Arcade Mode and set an official high score. From there the ladder is personal best, then Steam friends, then the global leaderboard.

The mode is unlockable rather than available from the start, so a new player's first session is Story Mode. The Steam listing also includes Steam Leaderboards and Stats among its features, which is how those scores are tracked and compared.

| Mode | How you unlock it | What you do | Competition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Story Mode | Available in Episode One | Fight through Storms with visual novel cutscenes between battles | None — narrative progression |
| Arcade Mode | Clear a Storm in Story Mode | Replay completed Storms for score | Personal bests, Steam friends, global leaderboards |

## How Single Player Combat Works

FIRESTARTERS is a movement shooter first. Kera's STEEL Battlesuit, custom rocket skates, and six-weapon arsenal are the whole toolkit, and the arenas double as skateparks: you skate, slide, grind, and ground pound through them while dodging mechanized claws.

The key solo-play twist is that there are no health, armor, or ammunition pickups in a STEEL Arena. You resupply by taking resources from defeated androids. As lead developer Alex Harte put it in press material around launch, there are no safety nets in the arenas of Steel and hunting is the path to survival.

In practice, that means defensive, backpedaling play runs out of resources. The single player design pushes you toward aggression because the enemies are the ammo crate. The game also rewards faster kills and higher scores, which is what feeds directly into Arcade Mode.

## Does FIRESTARTERS Have Co-op or Multiplayer?

Nothing in the official material describes cooperative or competitive multiplayer. The Steam app details list Single-player, Steam Achievements, Steam Cloud, Stats, Steam Leaderboards, Family Sharing, custom volume controls, stereo sound, and tags for playing at your own pace and without timed input — but no multiplayer or co-op category.

That is worth stating plainly rather than treating as a gap: as of the Early Access launch build in late October 2026, FIRESTARTERS is a solo game. Leaderboard chasing is the social layer. Whether that changes before 1.0 is not something the developers have confirmed, and the roadmap only commits to episodic story and gameplay content across four planned episodes.

## Early Access Details Solo Players Should Know

- **Platform:** PC via Steam, Windows only.
- **Price:** The Steam store page listed $9.99 at the time of writing. Pre-launch coverage noted the page showed no price at that point, so treat pricing as current-listing information rather than a permanent commitment.
- **Future pricing:** The studio says it plans to raise the price gradually as new content and features ship. Buying during Early Access includes all subsequent content updates before full release at no extra cost.
- **Demo:** A demo was available on Steam ahead of launch, per pre-release coverage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is FIRESTARTERS single player only?**
Yes. The Steam listing includes Single-player and does not list co-op or multiplayer categories, and no official material describes a multiplayer mode. The competitive element is score-based, through Arcade Mode leaderboards.

**Do I have to finish Story Mode before playing Arcade Mode?**
You need to clear a Storm in Story Mode at least once before that Storm becomes available in Arcade Mode. So the FIRESTARTERS single player loop starts with the story and opens up into score chasing.

**How much single player content is in the Early Access version?**
Episode One ships with the first part of Story Mode plus the unlockable Arcade Mode. The studio plans four major episodes in total before version 1.0, with an estimated 18 to 24 months in Early Access, though that figure may change. No official playtime estimate has been given.

**Can I play FIRESTARTERS without timed input or at my own pace?**
The Steam app details include tags for playing at your own pace and playing without timed input. Those are store categories rather than developer statements about specific difficulty or accessibility options, so check the current store page for the latest details.
