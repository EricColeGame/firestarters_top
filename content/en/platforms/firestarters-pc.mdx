## Is FIRESTARTERS Available on PC?

Yes. FIRESTARTERS launched on PC through Steam Early Access on October 28, 2026, and Steam's app data lists Windows as the only supported platform. Studio Kerosene develops and self-publishes the game, so there is no separate launcher or storefront to worry about — the [FIRESTARTERS Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3733710/FIRESTARTERS/) is the official place to buy it.

That single-platform status matters if you're weighing a purchase. There is no macOS or Linux build listed, and no console version appears in any of the material reviewed for this article. If you game on a desktop or laptop running Windows 10 or 11, you're in the supported lane.

## FIRESTARTERS PC System Requirements

The requirements below come straight from the Steam listing. Studio Kerosene explicitly labels both tiers as provisional — the notes say the specs "are NOT final and may change at any time," which is normal for a game that plans roughly 18 to 24 months of Early Access updates.

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| OS | Windows 10 (64-bit) | Windows 11 (64-bit) |
| Processor | 4-core CPU; Intel i3 or AMD Ryzen 3 | 6-core CPU; Intel i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 |
| Memory | 2 GB RAM | 2 GB RAM |
| DirectX | Version 12 | Not listed separately |
| Storage | 1 GB available space | 1 GB available space |

The most striking number is storage: 1 GB. That's not a typo. The developers attribute the small footprint to a custom engine built in-house from the ground up, and they advertise "no hard drive bloat" as a design goal.

### What the Low Specs Actually Mean

A 2 GB RAM floor and a 1 GB install size put FIRESTARTERS well below the hardware bar of most modern shooters. On paper, that widens the pool of laptops and older desktops that can run it. Just remember that these figures are self-reported and flagged as subject to change, so treat them as a starting point rather than a guarantee.

### Steam Deck Compatibility

Steam currently lists FIRESTARTERS' Steam Deck compatibility as "Unknown." That is not the same as unsupported — it means Valve hasn't published a rating yet. Since the game is an Early Access title with a fast, movement-heavy control scheme, checking back on the store page before buying for handheld play is the sensible move.

## What You Get in the Current Early Access Build

Episode One is the launch content. According to the developers, it contains the first part of the main Story Mode plus an unlockable Arcade Mode where players chase high scores on global leaderboards. They describe the base gameplay, story, and combat systems as "complete and functional," while noting they may change as more content lands.

The Steam page shows a post-launch update dated October 31, 2026, so the build is actively being patched.

- **Story Mode:** Kera, a former delinquent from Okinawa, fights as the designated competitor for a prisoner team called the Firestarters in STEEL, a livestreamed gladiator bloodsport run by a crime syndicate.
- **Arcade Mode:** Replay completed "Storms" to set official scores and climb global and friends leaderboards.
- **Movement kit:** A performance-boosting battlesuit plus custom rocket skates for skating, sliding, grinding, and ground-pounding.
- **Weaponry:** Six weapons, with no health, armor, or ammo pickups on the arena floor — you take resources from defeated androids instead.

Lead developer Alex Harte framed the design around aggression in an October 2026 announcement covered by MonsterVine: there are no safety nets in the arenas, and hunting is the path to survival.

## Price, Demo, and the Early Access Roadmap

The Steam store page displayed **$9.99** at the time of writing. Note that an October 24, 2026 preview from Kropp Media reported that no launch price was listed yet, so the figure may have been added closer to release. Either way, Studio Kerosene has been upfront about one thing: the price will go up.

Their stated plan is to raise the price gradually as new content and features ship. Anyone who buys during Early Access gets all subsequent content updates before the full 1.0 release for free.

On timing, the studio estimates 18 to 24 months in Early Access, with four total "Episodes" planned before version 1.0 — though they caution that number could change. MonsterVine reported on October 8, 2026 that a demo was available on Steam alongside a release-date trailer; the store listing reviewed here did not surface a demo button, so verify that yourself if trying before buying matters to you.

## Steam Features and Language Support

Beyond the game itself, the Steam listing confirms a standard but useful feature set for a single-player title:

- Steam Achievements and Steam Cloud saves
- Steam Leaderboards and player stats
- Family Sharing support
- Custom volume controls and stereo sound
- Tagged as playable at your own pace and playable without timed input

Language support is narrower than the feature list. English, Japanese, and Russian are each supported for interface and subtitles. No language is listed with full audio, which suggests the visual novel cutscenes are text-driven rather than voiced — consistent with the game's stated focus on story delivered through visual novel-style scenes.

One more thing worth flagging before you install: the store page carries a photosensitivity warning for flashing lights and stroboscopic effects, and the mature content descriptor covers blood, intense violence, strong language, mature and suggestive themes, and tobacco use.
