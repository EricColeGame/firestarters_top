FIRESTARTERS is a Windows PC game, and its Steam Early Access listing publishes two hardware tiers. The minimum calls for a 64-bit Windows 10 system with a 4-core Intel i3 or AMD Ryzen 3, 2 GB of RAM, DirectX 12, and 1 GB of storage; the recommended tier moves to Windows 11 and a 6-core Intel i5 or AMD Ryzen 5. Both lists carry a warning that the specs are not final and may change.

## FIRESTARTERS System Requirements at a Glance

Both tiers require a 64-bit processor and operating system. Here is the published comparison:

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| Operating system | Windows 10, 64-bit | Windows 11, 64-bit |
| Processor | 4-core Intel i3 or AMD Ryzen 3 | 6-core Intel i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 |
| Memory | 2 GB RAM | 2 GB RAM |
| DirectX | Version 12 | Not listed in the recommended block |
| Storage | 1 GB available space | 1 GB available space |
| Notes | Not final; may change | Not final; may change |

The listing does not name a specific GPU or VRAM amount. DirectX 12 appears under minimum, while the recommended block does not repeat a DirectX entry. That leaves no verified graphics-card cutoff to quote beyond the API note.

These figures come from the [official FIRESTARTERS Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3733710/FIRESTARTERS/), which is the best place to confirm them after an update.

## What Each Requirement Means for Your Build

### CPU and operating system

Windows 10 64-bit is the floor; Windows 11 64-bit is recommended. The CPU guidance is expressed as core count and product family—4-core Intel i3 or AMD Ryzen 3 at minimum, 6-core Intel i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 at recommended—not as exact model numbers. The listing does not identify a specific generation or benchmark score for either tier.

### Memory, DirectX, and storage

RAM stays at 2 GB in both tiers, so the recommended list does not raise the memory target. DirectX 12 is listed only for minimum. Storage is 1 GB available space in both tiers.

Studio Kerosene also says the game runs on an in-house custom engine built to avoid hard-drive bloat, AI upscaling, and shader compilation stutter. Treat that as a developer design claim, not a measured performance result.

## Platform Support: Windows, Steam Deck, and Languages

Steam lists Windows as the only platform for FIRESTARTERS. No macOS or Linux version is listed. Steam Deck compatibility is marked Unknown, so the page does not show a Verified or Playable rating.

The language table marks English, Japanese, and Russian for interface and subtitles; it does not mark a full-audio language. The same listing notes single-player support, Steam Achievements, Steam Cloud, leaderboards, and Family Sharing.

## Why the Specs Could Change Before 1.0

FIRESTARTERS launched in Steam Early Access on October 28, 2026, with Episode One. Studio Kerosene says Early Access will last until the final story episode, which it estimates at 18 to 24 months, and it plans four episodes before version 1.0.

Because the store page explicitly says the requirements are not final, the current numbers are a snapshot rather than a permanent floor. Recheck the listing after major content updates before buying hardware around it.
