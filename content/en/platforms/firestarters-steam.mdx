## What You Actually Play in Episode One

FIRESTARTERS describes itself as a fast-paced arcade movement shooter with visual novel story cutscenes. You play Kera, a former delinquent from Okinawa now imprisoned and assigned as the designated fighter for the Firestarters, the newest team in STEEL — a livestreamed gladiator bloodsport run by a Japanese crime syndicate. Between battles, the story unfolds through visual novel-style scenes.

The movement is the hook. Kera's battlesuit and custom rocket skates let you skate, slide, grind, and ground pound across arenas that double as skateparks, while android enemies try to claw you apart. Combat runs on six weapons.

The most distinctive rule: there are no health, armor, or ammunition pickups in a STEEL arena. You resupply by taking resources from defeated androids, which pushes you to keep hunting rather than retreat. Lead developer Alex Harte framed it plainly in the announcement coverage — there are no safety nets, and hunting is the path to survival.

Per the developer's own Early Access notes, Episode One ships the first part of the main Story Mode plus Arcade Mode, which unlocks so you can chase official high scores. The studio states that the base gameplay, story, and combat systems are already complete and functional, though all of it remains subject to change.

## Price, Episodes, and the Roadmap to 1.0

The Steam page currently lists FIRESTARTERS at $9.99. Studio Kerosene has said it plans to raise the price gradually as new content and features ship, and that anyone who buys during Early Access receives all subsequent content updates before the full release at no extra cost. Note that an earlier press write-up from late October reported that no launch price was posted at that time — the $9.99 figure is what the store page shows now, so treat pricing as something to re-check before you buy.

On content, the plan is four "Episodes" — major content releases — before version 1.0, with the studio cautioning that the number may change. Episode One is out; the remaining three are planned as free updates during Early Access. Version 1.0 arrives with the final story episode.

- **Episode One:** available now, includes Story Mode part one and Arcade Mode
- **Episodes Two through Four:** planned free updates during Early Access
- **1.0:** targeted for the final story episode, estimated 18–24 months out
- **Price movement:** expected to rise incrementally as episodes ship

The store page also shows a recent update dated October 31, so patch activity began within days of launch — worth watching if you prefer to wait for stability.

## Steam Features, Languages, and Deck Status

Beyond the game itself, the Steam listing confirms a fairly standard feature set for a single-player indie release. According to Steam's app details, FIRESTARTERS supports Steam Achievements, Steam Cloud, Stats, Steam Leaderboards, Family Sharing, custom volume controls, stereo sound, and is tagged as playable at your own pace and playable without timed input.

Language support is narrow. The store table lists English, Japanese, and Russian for both interface and subtitles, with no full audio track marked for any language. If you need another language, the current build does not offer it.

Steam Deck compatibility is the notable gap: the store page marks it as "Unknown," meaning Valve has not issued a verified or playable rating. There is no confirmed handheld status to report either way, so do not assume it runs well on Deck.

One technical point the developer emphasizes is the engine. FIRESTARTERS runs on a fully custom, in-house engine, and the studio pitches that as no hard drive bloat, no AI upscalers, and no shader compilation stutter. The listing also states no generative AI tools were used in its creation.

## PC System Requirements and the Demo

The requirements are unusually light, which fits a custom engine and a 1 GB install footprint. Steam labels all of these as non-final and subject to change.

**Minimum**
- OS: Windows 10 (64-bit)
- Processor: 4-core CPU such as Intel i3 or AMD Ryzen 3
- Memory: 2 GB RAM
- DirectX: Version 12
- Storage: 1 GB available space

**Recommended**
- OS: Windows 11 (64-bit)
- Processor: 6-core CPU such as Intel i5 or AMD Ryzen 5
- Memory: 2 GB RAM
- Storage: 1 GB available space

If you want to test performance before spending anything, a demo was announced as available on Steam ahead of launch, and press coverage from October 2026 confirmed it was live. That is the cheapest way to check whether the rocket-skate controls and the arena movement click for you.

Two content warnings worth knowing before you buy: the store page carries a photosensitivity warning for flashing lights and stroboscopic effects, and the mature content description cites blood, intense violence, strong language, mature and suggestive themes, and use of tobacco.
