## What a FIRESTARTERS Gameplay Video Actually Shows

If you are searching for a **FIRESTARTERS gameplay video** before spending money, the footage answers one question fast: this is a shooter built around never standing still. Studio Kerosene describes the game as a fast-paced arcade movement shooter with visual novel story cutscenes, and the recorded footage leans hard into that first word — fast.

Instead of holding angles behind cover, you skate, slide, grind, and ground pound through arenas that double as skateparks. Kera's kit is a performance-boosting STEEL Battlesuit, a custom pair of rocket skates, and a six-weapon arsenal of arena FPS guns. Watching a run is less about seeing enemies die and more about seeing how the player chains momentum between kills.

That distinction matters because FIRESTARTERS is in Steam Early Access, launched October 28, 2026, and priced at $9.99 on the [official FIRESTARTERS Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3733710/FIRESTARTERS/). Video is currently the cheapest way to judge whether the movement loop clicks for you.

## Movement, Weapons, and the No-Pickup Rule

The clearest mechanical hook in any FIRESTARTERS gameplay video is resource denial. There are no health, armor, or ammunition pickups in a STEEL arena. Everything you need comes from defeated androids — you take it rather than find it. Lead developer Alex Harte framed it plainly in the MonsterVine piece: the arenas have no safety nets, and hunting is how you survive.

That design pushes players forward instead of backward. Backpedaling to a health pack is not an option, so aggression becomes the efficient path. The six-weapon loadout gives you enough variety to handle the android horde, but the arenas — rails, ramps, sharp turns, mechanized claws — are what actually decide whether a run goes well.

The Coloured Static Press hands-on noted that runs move between bright, sun-bleached training spaces and dramatic red-lit industrial interiors, both designed for traversal rather than cover. That same preview flagged a legitimate concern: a dense HUD, stark visual effects, and multiple simultaneous warnings can compete for your attention once the action accelerates. Score feedback reads clearly; arena readability is the thing to watch as development continues.

## Story, Episodes, and the Arcade Mode Loop

FIRESTARTERS is not just combat rooms. The fiction casts you as Kera, a former delinquent from the back alleys of Okinawa now fighting as the designated competitor for a prisoner team, the Firestarters. The sport is STEEL, a livestreamed gladiator bloodsport run by a Japanese crime syndicate. Kera's motivation is freedom for her friends and atonement for her past, and visual-novel-style exchanges carry that story between battles.

Episode One, the current Early Access build, includes the first part of Story Mode plus an unlockable Arcade Mode. Clearing a Storm in the story unlocks the ability to replay it for an official high score, with personal bests, Steam friends, and a global leaderboard as the ladder.

Studio Kerosene's stated plan is four episodes total before version 1.0, with an estimated 18 to 24 months in Early Access. Early Access buyers receive subsequent content updates free, and the price is expected to rise as content ships — so the current $9.99 is the floor, not the ceiling.
