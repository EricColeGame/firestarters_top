## What FIRESTARTERS Actually Plays Like

The Steam page is unusually specific about the design, and it's the best source for setting expectations before you spend $9.99.

**The premise.** In the future, the world's most-watched livestream is STEEL, a Japanese gladiator bloodsport run by a notorious crime syndicate. You play Kera, a former delinquent from the back alleys of Okinawa who now fights as the designated competitor for a team of prisoners called the Firestarters. Her motivation is freedom for her new friends and atonement for her past. Story beats are delivered through visual novel cutscenes between battles.

**The movement.** Kera wears a performance-enhancing STEEL Battlesuit and a custom pair of rocket skates. Arenas double as skateparks: you skate, slide, grind, and ground pound across them while dodging mechanized claws. Movement isn't a garnish here — it's the survival mechanic.

**The combat.** Six weapons, no pickups. There are no health, armor, or ammo drops in a STEEL arena; resources come from the androids you kill. Lead developer Alex Harte framed it to MonsterVine as a design with no safety nets: "Hunting is the path to survival."

**The scoring layer.** Clearing an encounter ("Storm") the first time in Story Mode unlocks it in Arcade Mode, where you chase a personal best, then your Steam friends list, then the global leaderboard.

**The tech.** FIRESTARTERS runs on a custom in-house engine, and the studio states plainly that no generative AI tools were used in its creation — a claim it repeats in the store page disclaimers.

## Early Access Roadmap: Four Episodes, 18 to 24 Months

The developer's own Early Access FAQ lays out the plan more clearly than most.

- **Launch state:** Episode One only — the first part of Story Mode plus unlockable Arcade Mode. The studio describes the base gameplay, story, and combat systems as already complete and functional.
- **Planned scope:** four total "Episodes" (major content releases) before version 1.0, a number the studio says could change.
- **Timeline:** an estimated 18 to 24 months in Early Access, ending when the final story episode ships.
- **Pricing:** the plan is to raise the price gradually as content lands. Anyone who buys during Early Access gets all subsequent pre-1.0 content updates free.

MonsterVine's October 2026 report describes the three post-launch episodes as free updates during Early Access, which lines up with the store page's promise to early buyers.

## Price, Languages, and PC Requirements

At $9.99, FIRESTARTERS is priced like a small Early Access project, and the spec sheet matches — the footprint is tiny.

- **Interface and subtitles:** English, Japanese, Russian. No full audio language is listed on the store page.
- **Steam Deck:** compatibility listed as unknown.
- **Minimum:** Windows 10, 4-core CPU (Intel i3 or AMD Ryzen 3), 2 GB RAM, DirectX 12, 1 GB storage.
- **Recommended:** Windows 11, 6-core CPU (Intel i5 or AMD Ryzen 5), 2 GB RAM, 1 GB storage.

The developer explicitly notes that both requirement sets are not final and may change at any time — reasonable for a build that's expected to grow for up to two years.

Two content warnings worth flagging before purchase: the store page carries a photosensitivity warning for flashing lights and stroboscopic effects, and the mature content descriptor lists blood, intense violence, strong language, mature and suggestive themes, and tobacco use.
