## Gameplay Context Behind the Trailer

FIRESTARTERS is a fast-paced arcade movement shooter with visual novel story cutscenes, according to Steam. You play as Kera, a former delinquent from Okinawa who becomes the designated fighter for a prisoner team called the Firestarters. The team competes in STEEL, a livestreamed gladiator bloodsport run by a Japanese crime syndicate.

- **Movement:** Kera uses a performance-enhancing battlesuit and custom rocket skates. Steam describes skating, sliding, grinding, and ground-pounding through arenas that double as skateparks.
- **Combat:** The arsenal includes six weapons used against an android horde. There are no health, armor, or ammunition pickups in a STEEL arena; players are expected to take resources from combat rather than find them on the floor.
- **Story:** Visual novel cutscenes tell the story between battles. Kera’s stated motivation is earning freedom for her new friends and atoning for her past.
- **Replay:** Arcade Mode adds score chasing after the story introduces a Storm, with personal bests, Steam friends, and global leaderboards as targets.

Steam also says the game runs on a custom in-house engine and was made without generative AI tools. That detail is separate from the trailer, but it is part of the developer’s public positioning for the game.
