## Who Is the FIRESTARTERS Protagonist?

The FIRESTARTERS protagonist is **Kera**, a convict who fights as the designated competitor for the Firestarters, the newest prisoner team in a brutal televised bloodsport called STEEL. She is the character you control in both the story campaign and the score-chasing Arcade Mode that shipped with Episode One.

That matters because Kera is not a silent avatar. Studio Kerosene frames the entire game around her: her past as a street delinquent in Okinawa, her debts to the friends she now competes alongside, and her attempt to atone for what she did before prison. The visual-novel cutscenes between fights exist specifically to develop her.

## Kera's Backstory: From Okinawa's Back Alleys to the STEEL Arena

The official [FIRESTARTERS page on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3733710/FIRESTARTERS/) describes Kera as once having been a formidable delinquent street kid from the back alleys of Okinawa. By the time the game begins, she is a prisoner — and she has been assigned as her team's fighter rather than competing as an individual.

Her stated reasons for stepping into the arena are twofold. First, she is fighting to earn freedom for the friends she has made since becoming a prisoner. Second, she is fighting for atonement over her past sins — a phrase the developers use without spelling out exactly what those sins were.

That ambiguity appears intentional. Episode One covers only the opening portion of Story Mode, so any deeper history — family, the specific crime that led to her imprisonment, prior relationships — is not documented in the available material. Treat fan theories about her past as speculation until later episodes arrive.

## Inside STEEL: The Bloodsport That Owns the FIRESTARTERS Protagonist

To understand Kera's position, you need to understand the competition. STEEL is presented as the world's top-rated livestream in the game's futuristic setting: a Japanese gladiator bloodsport owned and operated by the nation's most notorious crime syndicate. It is entertainment first and a sport second.

That framing shapes how the protagonist is treated. As [MonsterVine's Early Access announcement coverage](https://monstervine.com/2026/10/firestarters-steam-early-access-october-28/) notes, the bloodsport values audience entertainment above the survival of its contestants. Kera is not a celebrated athlete climbing a ranked ladder — she is a prisoner whose continued participation depends on how watchable she is.

- **The team:** the Firestarters, a squad of inmates rather than free competitors.
- **The venue:** arenas described as resting places for the dead, repurposed by Kera into something closer to a skatepark.
- **The audience:** spectators who, per the developers, won't stand for stagnation and reward speed and high scores as much as survival.
- **The stakes:** freedom for her teammates, and personal redemption for Kera.

## How Kera Fights: Battlesuit, Rocket Skates, and Six Weapons

Kera's kit is the reason FIRESTARTERS plays like an arcade movement shooter rather than a cover-based one. She wears a performance-enhancing STEEL Battlesuit, straps on a custom-built pair of rocket skates, and carries an "expert-curated" suite of arena FPS weaponry — six weapons in total, according to the store description.

Movement verbs matter here. Players skate, slide, grind, and ground pound through each arena while dodging mechanized claws, and the spaces are built to be traversed rather than used as cover. The developers explicitly describe the arenas as a skatepark first.

There is also no safety net in the resource economy. STEEL arenas contain no health, armor, or ammunition pickups; instead, contestants take what they need from defeated androids. Lead developer Alex Harte framed it bluntly in the announcement: there are no safety nets in the arenas of Steel, and hunting is the path to survival. In practice, that means Kera has to keep pushing forward into enemies rather than retreating to recover.

## Story Delivery, Episodes, and What's Still Unconfirmed

Kera's story unfolds through visual novel–style cutscenes that bookend combat encounters. A [Coloured Static Press hands-on preview](https://colouredstaticpress.com/firestarters-hands-on-preview-steam-early-access-studio-kerosene/) from late October 2026 describes those exchanges as the device that introduces the cast and frames the action, and notes interest in whether later episodes will deepen the relationships and stakes. That's a first-impression observation from a short session, not a final verdict on the character writing.

On content cadence, the developers plan four episodes total before version 1.0, with an estimated 18 to 24 months in Early Access. Episode One includes the first part of Story Mode plus the unlockable Arcade Mode, where cleared Storms can be replayed for official high scores and global leaderboard placement. Episode One launched at $9.99 on Steam, with a UK price of £6.99 reported at launch, and the studio has said the price will rise as content ships.

A few honest caveats about the protagonist specifically:

- **No confirmed character arc beyond Episode One.** Kera's redemption story is clearly the spine of the campaign, but how it resolves has not been shown.
- **No named supporting cast in the available material.** Her teammates exist as a group; individual Firestarters members are not detailed in these sources.
- **No voice cast or performance details.** The Steam listing confirms English, Japanese, and Russian interface and subtitle support but does not list full audio for those languages.

## FAQ

**Who is the FIRESTARTERS protagonist?**
Kera, a former delinquent from Okinawa who is now a prisoner competing in the bloodsport STEEL. She serves as the designated fighter for the Firestarters, a team made up of fellow inmates.

**What is the FIRESTARTERS protagonist fighting for?**
Two things, per the developers: freedom for the friends she has made as a prisoner, and atonement for her past sins. The specific crime that landed her in prison has not been detailed in the released material.

**What weapons and gear does Kera use?**
A performance-enhancing STEEL Battlesuit, a custom pair of rocket skates, and a six-weapon arsenal of arena FPS guns. She cannot find health, armor, or ammo pickups in an arena — she has to take resources from defeated androids instead.

**Can you play as the FIRESTARTERS protagonist outside the story?**
Yes. After clearing a Storm for the first time in Story Mode, you can replay it in Arcade Mode to set an official high score and compete on global leaderboards. Episode One, which contains both modes, entered Steam Early Access on October 28, 2026.
