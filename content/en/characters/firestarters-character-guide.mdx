## Kera: The Protagonist You Actually Control

Kera is the anchor of the entire cast. According to the Steam page, she was "once a formidable delinquent street kid from the back alleys of Okinawa" before becoming a prisoner, and she now serves as the designated fighter for the Firestarters. Her stated motivations are twofold: earning freedom for the friends she has made on the team, and atoning for her past sins.

Everything you do in the arena is filtered through her kit:

- **A STEEL Battlesuit** that the store describes as performance-enhancing.
- **Custom-built rocket skates** that turn arenas into skateparks.
- **An arsenal of six weapons**, which the game frames as a curated suite of arena FPS hardware.

The hands-on preview from [Coloured Static Press](https://colouredstaticpress.com/firestarters-hands-on-preview-steam-early-access-studio-kerosene/) notes that the visual-novel-style dialogue scenes are what give the project personality beyond its mechanical influences — Kera is the character those scenes are built around.

## The Firestarters: A Team Built From Prisoners

The team name doubles as the game's title, and the premise is straightforward: STEEL's newest squad is made up entirely of inmates. Kera is the one who competes, and the official description frames her teammates as the reason she keeps going.

What is *not* confirmed matters just as much for anyone searching for a character list:

- No teammate names, designs, or fighting styles appear in the launch material.
- There is no published roster, relationship chart, or cast page from Studio Kerosene.
- No source indicates that teammates are playable or unlockable.

The Coloured Static Press preview says the outlet is interested in whether later episodes can "develop the relationships and stakes" — an expression of hope from a short session, not a confirmation that new named characters are coming. Treat any roster list you see elsewhere with suspicion until it traces back to Studio Kerosene.

## Who Kera Fights: Androids, Claws, and the Crowd

The opposition in FIRESTARTERS is described in collective terms. The Steam page refers to an "android horde" and to "savage mechanized claws," which suggests the enemies are treated as a swarm to be outmaneuvered rather than as personality-driven rivals with names and dialogue.

Combat design reinforces that reading. There are no health, armor, or ammunition pickups in a STEEL arena — you take what you need from defeated androids. Lead developer Alex Harte put it plainly in MonsterVine's [launch coverage](https://monstervine.com/2026/10/firestarters-steam-early-access-october-28/): "There are no safety nets in the arenas of Steel. Hunting is the path to survival."

The crowd is the third presence in every fight. STEEL spectators are written as a demanding audience that wants faster kills and higher scores, which is why clearing a Storm is only part of the challenge.

## How the Story Introduces Its Cast

FIRESTARTERS pairs its movement shooter core with visual novel story cutscenes, and those scenes are the primary vehicle for character work. The Steam description explicitly markets the game as "a fast-paced arcade movement shooter with visual novel story cutscenes," so expect dialogue-driven interludes between combat rather than in-arena banter.

Structure matters here too. Episode One, the current Early Access build, contains the first part of the main Story Mode plus the unlockable Arcade Mode. Completing a Storm for the first time in the story is what opens it up for score chasing on global leaderboards — the mode is the unlock, not a character.

## What Future Episodes Could Add to the Roster

Studio Kerosene plans four total episodes before version 1.0 and estimates 18 to 24 months in Early Access, with content shipped episodically alongside updates to existing material. Anyone buying in now receives subsequent content updates before full release at no extra cost, and the studio has said the price will rise gradually as new content lands.

That roadmap is the realistic place to expect character development — more story episodes mean more room for Kera's teammates and the syndicate to become actual people rather than descriptions. Studio Kerosene has not announced additional named characters, so any specific prediction about who appears in Episode Two or beyond is speculation.

One more note for players who care about production: the game runs on a custom in-house engine, and the studio states that no generative AI tools were used in its creation.

## FAQ

**Who is the main character in FIRESTARTERS?**
Kera, a former street delinquent from Okinawa who becomes a prisoner and the designated fighter for the Firestarters team in the STEEL bloodsport.

**Is there a complete FIRESTARTERS character list?**
No. As of Episode One, Kera is the only named character in official material. Her teammates, the syndicate operators, and the android enemies are described only as groups.

**Do I unlock characters by playing Story Mode?**
There is no verified evidence of unlockable characters. What Story Mode unlocks is Arcade Mode for each Storm, where you chase high scores on global leaderboards.

**What is STEEL in this FIRESTARTERS character guide?**
STEEL is the game's fictional Japanese gladiator bloodsport — the world's top-rated livestream, owned and operated by a crime syndicate. It is the setting that puts Kera and her team in the arena and the reason the crowd's demand for spectacle shapes how you play.
