## Kera: The Convict at the Center

### Her role in STEEL

Kera is the player character and the designated fighter for the Firestarters, the newest team in STEEL. The official listing describes her as a onetime formidable delinquent from the back alleys of Okinawa who now fights for the freedom of her newfound friends and atonement for her past sins. That gives the story a clear personal stake: her matches are not just about survival, but about earning freedom for people she now cares about.

What the launch material does not provide is a detailed personality profile or a full pre-prison biography beyond her Okinawan delinquent past. Her motives are clear, but much of her history is still left for the story to explore.

### Her gear defines the action

Kera’s kit is part of her character presentation. She uses a performance-enhancing STEEL Battlesuit, a custom pair of rocket skates, and arena FPS weaponry. The Steam listing ties those tools to skating, sliding, grinding, and ground-pounding through arenas, while her arsenal is described as six weapons. The design positions Kera as a movement-first fighter rather than a cover-shooter protagonist.

Her survivability is also tied to aggression. STEEL arenas do not place standard health, armor, or ammunition pickups; contestants take what they need from defeated androids. That mechanic reinforces the character’s framing as a prisoner who has to hunt to keep going.

## The Firestarters and the Forces of STEEL

### The Firestarters: a team, not a named roster

The Firestarters are the prisoner team Kera represents, described as the sport’s newest team. Kera is their designated fighter, and her motive includes earning freedom for her newfound friends. However, the official listing and launch coverage reviewed here do not name individual teammates, specify how many there are, or detail their backstories. For now, the Firestarters works as a collective story element: a group whose fate gives Kera a reason to compete.

That makes the team name important without turning it into a confirmed list of individually named characters. Readers looking for a full squad breakdown will have to wait for later story content.

### STEEL’s syndicate and android opposition

STEEL is the world’s top-rated livestream in the game’s future setting, a Japanese gladiator bloodsport run by a notorious crime syndicate. The competition is framed as valuing entertainment above the contestants’ survival, which makes the organization behind it an antagonistic force. The arenas are also filled with android enemies, including mechanized claws, and those enemies are the source of resources in combat.

Neither the syndicate leadership nor individual androids are named in the available material, so they should be treated as factions and enemy types rather than confirmed characters.

## How Episode One Introduces the Cast

Episode One is the current Early Access release. It includes the first part of Story Mode and an unlockable Arcade Mode. The story is delivered through visual novel-style cutscenes, which is where the cast gets context beyond combat and arena setup.

In a [Coloured Static Press hands-on preview](https://colouredstaticpress.com/firestarters-hands-on-preview-steam-early-access-studio-kerosene/), those visual-novel-style exchanges are described as bringing character and context between combat encounters, with anime-style story characters appearing in dialogue scenes. The preview also notes that later episodes could develop relationships and stakes; that is an observation about the story’s potential, not a confirmed plot summary.

The broader plan supports that reading. Studio Kerosene intends to ship four episodes in total before version 1.0, with the remaining three planned as free updates for Early Access owners, according to [MonsterVine’s Early Access launch report](https://monstervine.com/2026/10/firestarters-steam-early-access-october-28/). That structure means the launch cast is a starting point, but it does not confirm which new characters will appear or how Kera’s teammates will be developed.

## What Remains Unconfirmed About the FIRESTARTERS Characters

Several character questions are still open in the launch material:

- The names, count, and personalities of Kera’s Firestarters teammates.
- The identity and leadership of the crime syndicate behind STEEL.
- Individual names or lore for the android enemies.
- A full episode-by-episode cast list or confirmed future character additions.

Because FIRESTARTERS is in Early Access, the official listing warns that content is subject to change. The base gameplay, story, and combat systems are described as complete and functional, but the developers say they may evolve as new episodes are developed. Any roster list that presents unnamed teammates or syndicate figures as confirmed characters is getting ahead of the available evidence.

## FAQ

### Who is the main character in FIRESTARTERS?

Kera is the main character and player protagonist. She is a former Okinawan delinquent turned prisoner who fights as the designated competitor for the Firestarters in STEEL.

### Are Kera’s Firestarters teammates named?

Not in the official Steam listing or the launch coverage reviewed here. They are described collectively as a team of prisoners and Kera’s newfound friends, but individual names and backstories are not confirmed.

### Is STEEL a character?

No. STEEL is the gladiator bloodsport and livestream that hosts the competition. Its crime syndicate operators and android enemies are antagonistic forces, but they are not named characters in the launch material.

### Will later episodes add more FIRESTARTERS characters?

Studio Kerosene plans four episodes in total, with Episode One already available in Early Access and three more planned as free updates. The structure leaves room for more story and cast development, but no specific future character names are confirmed in the available material.
