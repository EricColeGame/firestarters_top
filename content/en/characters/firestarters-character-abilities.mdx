## What "Character Abilities" Means in FIRESTARTERS Right Now

If you searched for FIRESTARTERS character abilities expecting a roster page with stat blocks and unlock trees, here is the honest answer: in the current Early Access build, the game documents one playable fighter — Kera — and her "abilities" are really a movement-and-weapon kit rather than a list of named powers. Studio Kerosene's [official FIRESTARTERS page on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3733710/FIRESTARTERS/) describes her loadout as a performance-enhancing STEEL Battlesuit, a custom pair of rocket skates, and a curated suite of arena FPS weaponry.

That matters because FIRESTARTERS is a movement shooter first. Your survival and your score come from how you chain skating, sliding, grinding, and shooting, not from picking a class with a cooldown bar. Everything below is drawn from the Steam listing and from launch-window coverage published between October 7 and October 30, 2026.

## Kera's Core Kit: Battlesuit, Skates, and Six Weapons

Kera is the designated fighter for the Firestarters, a team of prisoners competing in STEEL, a Japanese gladiator bloodsport run by a crime syndicate. Her background as a delinquent from Okinawa is delivered through visual-novel-style cutscenes between battles rather than through ability unlocks.

Her confirmed equipment breaks into three parts:

- **STEEL Battlesuit** — described by the developer as performance-enhancing, and the reason the store page frames her movement as "best-in-class."
- **Custom rocket skates** — the source of the speed that defines every encounter.
- **Six-weapon arsenal** — the store page specifies an arsenal of six weapons, curated for arena combat.

Notably, the Steam description says this movement and "fully self-sufficient gunplay" are granted to all players, which reads as a design statement that the kit is fixed rather than chosen from a menu of characters with wildly different power sets.

## Movement Abilities: Skate, Slide, Grind, Ground Pound

The closest thing FIRESTARTERS has to a formal ability list is its traversal vocabulary. The Steam page tells players to "skate, slide, grind, and ground pound" through each arena, treating the blood-stained stages as a skatepark rather than a series of rooms to hold.

That reframes what counts as a character ability here:

- **Skating** is your baseline state. The Coloured Static Press hands-on preview notes that arenas of rails, ramps, and sharp turns are designed to be traversed, not used for cover.
- **Sliding and grinding** keep momentum alive across those rails and ramps.
- **Ground pound** appears as a movement verb in the same sentence as the others, so treat it as traversal — not a confirmed damage skill with a listed radius or cooldown.

The same preview mentions a HUD "synchronisation meter" visible during play, alongside tracked enemy counts, score, and multipliers. Its exact function is not explained in any available material, so it is worth watching rather than assuming.

## Combat Abilities and the Aggression Economy

FIRESTARTERS has no health, armor, or ammunition pickups in a STEEL arena. The developer's line is blunt: you don't need them, and "if the world doesn't give, you'll take." In practice, that means your sustain comes from defeated androids — resources are tied directly to kills, as Noisy Pixel's launch coverage explains.

Lead developer Alex Harte framed it to MonsterVine as "no safety nets" in the arenas, with hunting as the path to survival. Two consequences follow for how you play:

1. **Standing still starves you.** Without pickups, retreating to recover is not an option the map supports.
2. **Speed and scoring are the same skill.** Clear a Storm and the game still pushes you to slay faster and score higher, because STEEL's spectators demand it.

The Coloured Static preview specifically praised this loop for giving players a reason to retry arenas instead of treating each one as a checklist of targets.

## What Still Isn't Confirmed About the Character Roster

Early Access launched October 28, 2026 with Episode One, which includes the first part of Story Mode plus an unlockable Arcade Mode with global leaderboards. Studio Kerosene plans four episodes total before version 1.0, estimating roughly 18 to 24 months in Early Access.

What that means for abilities research:

- **No verified alternate fighters.** The material names only Kera as the player character. The "Firestarters" are her team of prisoners, but their playability is not stated anywhere in the sources reviewed.
- **No published ability trees, upgrades, or stat screens.** If Episode One contains them, none of the available coverage documents them.
- **Expect change.** The developer states that the base gameplay, story, and combat systems are complete and functional but "subject to change" as new content ships.

If you want to track this yourself, the Steam listing's update feed is the reliable place to watch — it showed a post-launch update on October 31, 2026.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does FIRESTARTERS have multiple playable characters with different abilities?**
Not according to the current material. Kera is the only fighter identified as playable, and her kit — battlesuit, rocket skates, and six weapons — is presented as universal rather than selectable. Future episodes could change that, but nothing has been confirmed.

**How many weapons does Kera carry in FIRESTARTERS?**
Six. The official Steam description specifies an arsenal of six devastating weapons, used against the android horde in each arena.

**Are FIRESTARTERS character abilities tied to pickups or upgrades?**
There are no health, armor, or ammo pickups in a STEEL arena. Resources are earned by defeating enemies, which pushes players toward constant movement and aggressive play rather than map scavenging.

**What movement abilities should I practice first?**
Start with skating and grinding, since momentum is the foundation of the whole kit. Sliding and ground pounding are listed alongside them on the store page, and the arenas are explicitly built as skateparks with rails, ramps, and turns to chain together.
